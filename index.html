What it does

A capture rig, not a toy.

01 · FLASH

Browser USB flasher →

Install or update from Chrome/Edge over USB — pick a board and version, click Install. No esptool, no drivers.
02 · CAPTURE

Channel-hopping engine

Sweeps 2.4 and 5 GHz, deauths eligible APs to force reconnects, and grabs EAPOL 4-way handshakes and PMKIDs — with live per-event stats.

03 · CRACK

Cloud cracking

Captures reach wpa-sec, OnlineHashCrack and PwnCrack — directly over the board's own WiFi, or through one small relay you host that fans them out and merges the results.

04 · RESULT

Password + join QR

Cracked passwords are written back to the device and shown on the capture, with a scannable WIFI: join QR.

05 · KEEP

Registry & excludes

Every network is remembered by SSID even after you delete the capture — so it stays listed, and never-attack excludes stick across radios and reboots.

06 · CONTROL

BLE phone portal

A web page over Bluetooth configures and syncs the board — no access point, no board WiFi uplink. It's the entire UI on the single-button boards (Waveshare, Cardputer ADV, T-Dongle S3).

How it works

Capture → crack → password back.

Grab handshakes on the device, get them to the crackers, and the recovered passwords land right back on the board. There are two ways to reach the crackers.

Device

Capture

handshakes + PMKIDs

──▶
Upload

Direct · or relay

board WiFi, or your phone via a relay

──▶
Cloud

Crackers

wpa-sec · OHC · PwnCrack

Direct — a button board uploads straight over its own WiFi; nothing else to set up. Relay (optional) — a small host you deploy once lets the board hit a single endpoint that fans out to all three services and merges results, and lets your phone sync the board over Bluetooth (the only way on the single-button Waveshare / Cardputer ADV / T-Dongle S3). Cracked passwords flow back either way.

Cracking options

Cloud services — or crack it yourself.

All free. A key from any one cloud service is enough to start — or skip the cloud and crack the hashes yourself, offline.

WPA-SEC

wpa-sec →

Stanev's community distributed WPA cracker. Free; BBoink uploads the .pcap and reads back the cracked passwords.
OHC

OnlineHashCrack →

GPU cracking service with a free tier and an API — BBoink submits WPA 22000 hashes.
PWNCRACK

PwnCrack →

Free handshake cracking service — BBoink uploads hc22000 and reads back the cracked passwords.
LOCAL

Crack it yourself →

No account, fully offline — download the hashes from the BLE portal and run hashcat -m 22000 on your own machine.
